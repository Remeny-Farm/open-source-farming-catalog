# Tin can cigarette butt collector

A 3D-printed lid that turns an ordinary tin can into an outdoor cigarette butt bin. The lid sits on the open can; butts drop through the opening into the metal can, so embers meet metal rather than plastic. The built example is mounted on a wooden post at Remény Farm.

## State

- **Field-tested**: the unit in the photo is in outdoor use on the farm. The duration of use, load and weathering have not been recorded or measured.
- Only an STL is available. There is no editable CAD source; the STL is the design.
- The printed part is flammable. Butts must be extinguished before disposal.

## Files

| Purpose | Path |
|---|---|
| Print the lid | `export/tin-can-cigarette-butt-collector.stl` |
| Bill of materials | `bom.csv` |

The STL's bounding box is 172 × 194 × 178 mm. Check it against your can before printing; the fit has only been tried on the can in the photo.

## Building it

1. Print the lid. The built example is black; the filament type and print settings were not recorded.
2. Empty and clean a tin can and remove its lid completely.
3. Fit the printed lid onto the can and fix the assembly where people smoke, away from dry vegetation.
